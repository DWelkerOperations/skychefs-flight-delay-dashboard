Definitions and Caveats

Loading snapshot coverage. Loading period completeness. Hourly analysis groups by scheduled local hour. Hourly and period average delay is total valid delay minutes divided by valid movements after matching movements are aggregated. Positive values are late; negative values are early. Missing actual times and timestamp differences outside −180 to +1,440 minutes are excluded. Airline is the two-character marketed flight-number prefix and may include codeshares. Location uses the first three station-label characters to merge aliases such as ORD and ORD NEW (MC5). Times are local as recorded.
