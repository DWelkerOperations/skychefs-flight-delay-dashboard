Definitions and caveats

This static snapshot covers scheduled movements from February 1 through July 20, 2026. Weekly periods are Sunday–Saturday; July 19–20 is a partial week. July is also a partial month. On-time performance means less than 15 minutes late. Hourly analysis groups by scheduled local hour; monthly hourly rates aggregate matching movements before calculating the percentage. Rates use valid, deduplicated movements as the denominator. Missing actual times and timestamp differences outside −180 to +1,440 minutes are excluded. Airline is the two-character marketed flight-number prefix and may include codeshares. Location uses the first three station-label characters to merge aliases such as ORD and ORD NEW (MC5). Times are local as recorded.
